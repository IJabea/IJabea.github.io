Awards

1-  University College Alumni Association Teaching Assistant Award: This award recognizes outstanding teaching ability and service to students and acknowledges and emphasizes the important role teaching assistants play in providing quality academic instruction. This award is presented annually to only two graduate students across LSU.

2- David Oxley Graduate Student Teaching Award: A prestigious award recognizing exceptional teaching by graduate students. Presented each semester to no more than one outstanding graduate instructor in recognition of excellence in undergraduate teaching and instructional effectiveness.
