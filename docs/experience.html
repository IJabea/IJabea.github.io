Awards

Recognition for teaching, mentorship, and service to students.

University College Alumni Association Teaching Assistant Award

This award recognizes outstanding teaching ability and service to students and emphasizes the important role teaching assistants play in providing quality academic instruction. It is presented annually to only two graduate students across LSU.

David Oxley Graduate Student Teaching Award

A prestigious award recognizing exceptional teaching by graduate students. Presented each semester to no more than one outstanding graduate instructor in recognition of excellence in undergraduate teaching and instructional effectiveness.

Certificate of Teaching Excellence

Awarded by the Department of Mathematics at Louisiana State University.
